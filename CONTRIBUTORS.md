# Contributors

devin-2api lite is a trimmed, partially rewritten fork of
[leookun/devin-2api](https://github.com/leookun/devin-2api). Credit belongs to the people below.

## Original project

- **leookun** ([@leookun](https://github.com/leookun)) — author of
  [devin-2api](https://github.com/leookun/devin-2api): protocol reverse engineering, field
  semantics, original adapter and codecs. Most of the `internal/llm`, `internal/api` and
  `internal/adapter/devin` design comes from that work (MIT, © 2026 leookun).

## This fork

- **Green-hats** ([@Green-hats](https://github.com/Green-hats)) — lite maintainer: trimming,
  hand-written protobuf + Connect layer, model catalog/snapshot/manifest, tests and docs.
